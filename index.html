Toolkit

You don’t need hundreds. You need about eight.

What freelancers and small creative studios keep recommending is a small stack: one tool per job, free native tools first, and one AI assistant that connects to the rest. This is that, for what you’re building.

The BrainClaude (Cowork) — CONNECTED

Builds pages and apps, writes, researches, checks email and Drive, runs scheduled jobs. Pro is A$34 a month; watch the extra-usage top-ups.

The TruthGoogle Drive, Gmail, Calendar — CONNECTED

Every file and brand kit lives in Drive. Gmail feeds the email check. Calendar is where the family events go.

DesignCanva — CONNECTED

Posts, carousels, resizes and brand checks. Brand Kit needs Canva Pro, so check whether your plan has it before paying.

VideoCapCut Pro — A$2.99/MO

Edit Reels, TikToks and hands-only SOFTR clips. Cheap and enough, so skip Descript, OpusClip and HeyGen for now.

AI Images & VideoPick one: Higgsfield or OpenArt — CHOOSE

Both are connected. Higgsfield is stronger for ad video but its payment is failing and credits don’t roll over. OpenArt starts cheaper. Paying for both makes no sense.

Posting & AdsMeta Business Suite + Ads Manager — FREE

Free scheduling for Instagram and Facebook, and where every ad runs. Keep audiences broad and test a few creatives.

HostingRailway — ~US$17/MO

Runs the City Ink app and Yumi. HQ reads both live from here. Most of the bill is memory, so shut down idle services.

Shop & WaitlistShopify — RECONNECT

You already have a store started for SOFTR. Reconnect it in Claude before using it for the waitlist.

Glue (only when needed)Zapier — FREE TIER

For one-off “when this happens, do that” jobs. Skip Make and n8n unless Zapier’s free plan runs out.

Already installed. Just ask for these

  • “Make me a week of posts for City Ink” — small-business › social content engine, then Canva to design
  • “Are my ads working? What should I turn off?” — small-business › ad manager (works from a Meta CSV export)
  • “Write a proposal for a new client, prepaid 3 months” — small-business › proposal builder
  • “Chase the invoices nobody’s paid” — small-business › invoice chase, from Gmail
  • “Plan the SOFTR waitlist campaign” — marketing › campaign plan, then SOFTR Brand Book prompts
  • “Resize this Canva post for stories and feed” — canva › resize for social media
  • “Check this is on brand for Runnit” — brand-voice and canva › brand check
  • “Make a doc / deck for Danny” — Claude Docs or Slides, export to Word, PDF or PowerPoint

Turn off or skip

  • Second Brain (weekly), Lumi, Time Atlas: they do the same job as HQ, Crew OS and Claude. Second Brain alone is about $56 a month.
  • SecondBrain Pro auto-renew: paid to Aug 2027, so just switch off the renewal.
  • Google AI Plus 2 TB: you already pay for iCloud+ 2 TB. Keep one.
  • AI Advantage Club: payment failed but it’s still open. Cancel it properly.
  • Extra app builders: Lovable, Replit and Emergent. Claude builds your pages; keep Base44 only for OmniCore and Timeliner.
  • Big-spender ad tools: Motion analytics and Windsor.ai paid plans aren’t worth it until you’re spending real money on ads.
  • Supermemory and Notion: you’d already planned to drop Supermemory, and Notion is only a view. Drive plus HQ covers both.
  • Postiz, Make, n8n: Meta Business Suite and Zapier’s free plan cover posting and glue for now.
  • Two rules: if you haven’t opened a tool on your own in 7 days, cancel it. No new subscription until it’s added to Money above.
